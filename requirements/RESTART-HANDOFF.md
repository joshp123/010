## Why the previous approach failed

These are hypotheses/evidence boundaries for independent reassessment, not instructions to endorse another patch:

- Unconstrained drawing edits moved or invented physical features. Prompting “preserve everything” did not enforce correspondence.
- The opposite local minimum was literal photo/CAD material with tiny generated changes. Filling gaps or changing shaders did not make a convincing miniature.
- Broad teacher-to-binding loss was initially blamed incorrectly: matched-scale roof crops showed stains/hardware already present in the drawing. Removing original photo building layers did not fix that. Grazing-angle overwrite was a separate, real defect and was corrected.
- Semantic reconstruction improved style by discarding source evidence, then invented plausible replacements. Labels and generated masks require verification; they are not ground truth.
- Source identity, material interpretation, artistic coherence, cross-view binding and delivery performance were repeatedly conflated. Separate their failure causes without losing responsibility for the complete experience.
- Too much effort went into packaging, evidence bundles, repeated manager messages, tiny experiments and unaccepted local minima. Passing builds, counts and output sizes do not justify continuation or acceptance.
- Per-UV-island MaxRects packing became an unnecessary long compilation bottleneck. Valid shelf placement already existed; atlas layout reuse helped texture-only revisions. Check actual payload/decoded memory, not packing cleverness.
