Geographic data for 010

Municipal boundary: Kadaster / PDOK, Bestuurlijke Gebieden, CC BY 4.0.
https://api.pdok.nl/kadaster/brk-bestuurlijke-gebieden/ogc/v1

Overview terrain and water: Kadaster / PDOK, BRT TOP10NL, CC BY 4.0.
https://api.pdok.nl/kadaster/brt-top10nl/ogc/v1
The source geometry was clipped to Rotterdam, classified and simplified for display.

Public destinations: PDOK Locatieserver, using Kadaster BAG and CBS place records.
Every destination retains a source URL and record ID.

Detailed 3D model: Gemeente Rotterdam. Catalogue license: public domain.
https://www.rotterdam.nl/rotterdam-in-3d
https://data.overheid.nl/dataset/10230-rotterdam-3d

Aerial imagery used for source research and validation: Samenwerkingsverband
Beeldmateriaal / PDOK, Luchtfoto 2026, CC BY 4.0. Raw photographs are not
displayed as the miniature's streets.
https://www.pdok.nl/introductie/-/article/pdok-luchtfoto-rgb-open-

CC BY 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
These source-data notices are separate from the MIT license for application code.

Large yards, paving and crane tracks: Kadaster / PDOK BGT, CC0 1.0.
https://api.pdok.nl/kadaster/bgt/ogc/v1
See industrial-ground/manifest.json for source inventory and display geometry.

Street/path/rail tiles: Kadaster / PDOK BRT TOP10NL, CC BY 4.0. Source and transformation manifest: streets/manifest.json.
Surrounding land and water: Kadaster / PDOK BRT TOP10NL, CC BY 4.0. Simplified
context outside the exact municipality. Source and transformation details:
source-data/context-land.json and docs/context-land.md in the repository.

Prepared streetlights: source positions and geometry from Gemeente Rotterdam,
Rotterdam 3D public-domain model. Straight shafts and emitted light are illustrated.
See lamps/manifest.json for source inventory and processing details.

Prepared transit routes/trip links: OVapi / Stichting OpenGeo, GTFS NL, CC0 1.0.
Transit shape database: OVapi / Stichting OpenGeo, GTFS NL, ODbL 1.0.
https://gtfs.ovapi.nl/README
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
The municipal derivative is available in transit/map.pb.gz under ODbL 1.0;
retain attribution and database share-alike terms. It is clipped to Kadaster /
PDOK GM0599 (CC BY 4.0), without simplification, and quantised to 1e-7 degrees.
The original table-specific licence, source hashes and rebuild transform are
available in source-data/transit and scripts/build-transit.py in the project
repository. These terms are separate from the MIT application code licence.
Prepared geometry is not an observed vehicle position or measured tunnel depth.

Metro physical-form subset (transit/metro-physical.json.gz): Kadaster / PDOK,
BRT TOP10NL, CC BY 4.0. Extracted from the retained municipality-clipped rail
network; physical forms classified, source IDs retained. Plan geometry only;
application tunnel depth is illustrative. Source/rebuild details:
source-data/transit/presentation.md and scripts/build-metro-physical.py.

Simulated road traffic paths (traffic/roads.pb.gz): Rijkswaterstaat / PDOK,
Nationaal Wegenbestand, CC0 1.0. Municipality clipping and BRT tunnel exclusion
use Kadaster / PDOK CC BY 4.0 sources. Source vertices/junctions retained;
clipping, tunnel subtraction and coordinate quantisation described in
source-data/traffic/README.md. Cars, speeds and density are simulated, not
live observations. Independent renderer/build code remains MIT.

Measured port forms: Actueel Hoogtebestand Nederland (AHN) / Het Waterschapshuis — AHN6, CC BY 4.0. Selected survey flights from December 2025; these are dated structures and objects, not live vessel reports.
