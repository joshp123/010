Explore the whole municipality, from the Maas to the North Sea. Real buildings, a miniature point of view, and windows that glow after dark.

Live follows Rotterdam’s time and current modelled weather. Day, Dusk and Night let you linger in a different light.

3D city model: Gemeente Rotterdam. Current textured buildings and tank edition: 3D Rotterdam, CC BY 4.0; prepared for this miniature. Boundary, map and place data: Kadaster / PDOK, CC BY 4.0. Port forms: AHN / Het Waterschapshuis, CC BY 4.0, December 2025 survey. Weather: Open-Meteo. Building data is a snapshot; it may differ from the city today. Night lighting is illustrated.

Vehicle positions: OVapi / NDOV and NS via NL Trains Live (CC0). Timetable route names: NDOV, CC0; shape database: ODbL. Raw positions and times are preserved. Underground metros may use a labelled approximate position on a compatible track; they do not move between reports. Grey vehicles have stale, unknown-time or disconnected reports. Metro tunnel plan: Kadaster / PDOK BRT, CC BY 4.0. Its depth and vehicle dimensions are illustrated.

Simulated road journeys: Rijkswaterstaat NWB / WKD, CC0. Selected turn restrictions: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Source coverage is incomplete; decorative traffic is not a route-planning service.
