Delfshaven, three ways

One 3D model of a square kilometre of Delfshaven, built from Rotterdam's open data, drawn three ways. Same place, same light. Open each on your phone.

Live 3D
The model drawn in your browser as pixel art. Rotates to any angle. Tap px for the unpixelated model.
Pixel pyramid
The same drawing rendered ahead of time into image tiles. Four angles.
Painted
The pixel pyramid repainted by an image model, with rails and road markings put back from data. One angle.
